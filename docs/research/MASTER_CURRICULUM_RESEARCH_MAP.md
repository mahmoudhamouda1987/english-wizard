# Master Curriculum Research Map

## CEFR
**Primary reference:** Council of Europe, CEFR Companion Volume (2020) and current CEFR resource ecosystem.
**Use:** capability descriptors, mediation, online interaction, communicative language activities and progression.
**Rule:** English Wizard internal estimates are learning estimates, not official CEFR certification.

## IELTS
**Primary reference ecosystem:** IELTS official assessment and band-descriptor resources.
**Use:** exam task familiarity, timing, rubric-aware preparation and internal readiness estimates.
**Rule:** English Wizard does not claim to award official IELTS scores or certification.

## Cambridge English
**Primary reference ecosystem:** Cambridge English qualification specifications and assessment resources.
**Use:** qualification-specific practice and internal readiness mapping.
**Rule:** preparation is separate from official examination/certification.

## Lexical resources
Use verified dictionary/corpus resources for lexical meaning, register, pronunciation and collocation evidence where rights permit. Store provenance and rights state for reusable content.

## Learning science
The learning engine explicitly supports retrieval, spacing, interleaving, productive recall, transfer and delayed review. Research references used for curriculum decisions must be recorded in the content-governance/reference layer before production claims are made.

## Research-to-curriculum chain
CEFR descriptor → capability → sub-capability → skill → language resource → assessment → mastery criterion → mission → review/retention requirement.

## Provenance requirement
Every reusable production content item must have origin, rights, review status, target level, objective, safety/answer/ambiguity/difficulty/grammar/facts checks and source references before publication.
