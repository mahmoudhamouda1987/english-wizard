# English Wizard — Curriculum & Assessment Research Pack

## Purpose

This pack records the external reference systems that English Wizard uses as its educational backbone. It does not reproduce protected source material; it records provenance, implementation consequences and links for traceability.

## 1. CEFR / Council of Europe

Primary reference: Council of Europe CEFR online and the 2020 Companion Volume.

- CEFR is the principal proficiency framework for the product.
- The Companion Volume (2020) updates and extends the 2001 CEFR and provides the illustrative descriptor system, including mediation and plurilingual/pluricultural dimensions.
- English Wizard must map internal learning objectives to CEFR descriptors without presenting internal estimates as official CEFR certification.
- Assessment design must preserve the distinction between learning evidence and certification.

Sources:
- Council of Europe CEFR descriptors: https://www.coe.int/en/web/common-european-framework-reference-languages/cefr-descriptors
- Council of Europe CEFR documents: https://www.coe.int/en/web/common-european-framework-reference-languages/documents
- Council of Europe CEFR Companion Volume 2020: https://rm.coe.int/cefr-companion-volume-with-new-descriptors-2020/16809ea0d4

## 2. Cambridge English

Cambridge English Qualifications are separate examinations aligned to CEFR levels. They are assessment products, not interchangeable with the English Wizard general-learning pathway.

Implementation consequence:
- Cambridge pathways must have their own objective/task/rubric/format models.
- English Wizard may prepare a learner for a Cambridge qualification, but must not imply that in-app performance is a Cambridge result or certificate.
- Cambridge exam preparation should use official specifications and appropriately licensed/original content.

Sources:
- Cambridge international language standards / CEFR: https://www.cambridgeenglish.org/exams-and-tests/cefr/
- Cambridge English Qualifications: https://www.cambridgeenglish.org/exams-and-tests/qualifications/
- Cambridge comparison guidance: https://support.cambridgeenglish.org/hc/en-gb/articles/202838386-Comparing-Cambridge-English-Qualifications-to-other-exams

## 3. IELTS

IELTS is treated as a distinct assessment pathway. Its score scale, task format and assessment purpose are not collapsed into the CEFR learning model.

Implementation consequence:
- General English mastery remains CEFR-based.
- IELTS preparation uses an explicit exam pathway, exam-specific tasks, score-oriented practice and exam rubrics.
- No English Wizard score is presented as an official IELTS band or test result.

Source to verify before launch of the pathway:
- IELTS official site: https://ielts.org/

## 4. Content and licensing policy

Every persisted learning-content item carries provenance and approval metadata. Content must be classified as original, licensed, external, AI-generated or reference. External/reference material is never silently treated as owned product content.

## 5. Research-to-curriculum traceability

Each future curriculum objective should point to:

`framework -> descriptor/evidence -> capability -> skill -> activity -> assessment -> mastery evidence`

This is the research provenance chain required before a curriculum item is promoted to production.

## 6. Important boundary

The research pack is evidence and provenance, not a claim that English Wizard has completed a formal external validation study. Formal validation and high-stakes assessment require separate review.
